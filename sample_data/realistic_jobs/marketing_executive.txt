Job Title: Marketing Executive
Company: Tadka Foods
Location: New Delhi, India (on-site)
Employment type: Full-time

About us:
Tadka Foods makes ready-to-cook Indian meals sold in 3,000 stores and online. We are
known for bold flavours and bolder campaigns.

About the role:
We are hiring a Marketing Executive to plan and run brand campaigns for our food and
lifestyle products. You will grow our social media, write copy and organize launch events.

Responsibilities:
- Plan and run social media marketing on Instagram, YouTube and other channels
- Write engaging copy for posts, packaging and campaigns
- Organize product launch events and in-store activations
- Work with designers, agencies and the sales team
- Report campaign results every month

Requirements:
- 2+ years of experience in marketing
- Social media marketing and brand strategy
- Copywriting
- Event planning
- Strong communication and public speaking skills
- Leadership to coordinate several teams

Nice to have:
- Excel for campaign reporting
- Google Analytics
- Content marketing or email marketing experience

What we offer:
- Health insurance
- Free product every month
- Performance bonus

How to apply:
Send your resume and a campaign you are proud of.
