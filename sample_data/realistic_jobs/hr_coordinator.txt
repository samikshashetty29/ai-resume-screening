Job Title: HR Coordinator
Company: Northstar Logistics
Location: Hyderabad, India (on-site)
Employment type: Full-time

About us:
Northstar Logistics runs warehouses and delivery fleets for online retailers, with 900
employees across 6 cities.

About the role:
We are hiring an HR Coordinator to support recruitment, onboarding and employee
engagement. You will be the first point of contact for many employee questions.

Responsibilities:
- Schedule interviews and coordinate with hiring managers
- Run onboarding so new joiners are ready on day one
- Maintain accurate employee records
- Organize training sessions and engagement events
- Answer payroll queries with the finance team

Requirements:
- 2+ years of experience in HR coordination
- Recruitment and onboarding
- Payroll support
- Employee engagement
- Communication and attention to detail
- Bachelor's degree; an MBA in HR is an advantage

Nice to have:
- Experience with HR software such as Zoho People or Keka
- Excel
- Time management across several priorities

What we offer:
- Health insurance
- Paid leave and festival bonus
- Training and growth into an HR generalist role

Send your resume through our careers page.
