Job Title: Financial Analyst
Company: Rangoli Home Stores
Location: Ahmedabad, India (on-site)
Employment type: Full-time

About us:
Rangoli Home Stores sells furniture and home decor through 60 stores in western India.

About the role:
We are looking for a Financial Analyst to support budgeting and forecasting across our
retail business. You will prepare monthly reports for senior managers and help them
understand costs and margins.

Responsibilities:
- Prepare the annual budget and monthly forecasts
- Build financial models for new stores and pricing decisions
- Run variance analysis and explain the results
- Prepare reports on costs and margins for senior managers
- Support the yearly audit

Requirements:
- 3+ years of experience in financial analysis
- Strong Excel and financial modeling skills
- Budgeting and forecasting experience
- Accounting knowledge
- Attention to detail and clear communication
- Bachelor's degree in finance, accounting or commerce

Nice to have:
- Power BI or Tableau
- SQL
- Auditing or taxation knowledge
- CFA or CA progress

What we offer:
- Health insurance
- Yearly bonus
- Support for professional exams

How to apply:
Send your resume and your notice period.
