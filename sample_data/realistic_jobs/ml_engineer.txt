Job Title: Machine Learning Engineer
Company: Saransh AI Labs
Location: Hyderabad, India (hybrid)
Employment type: Full-time

About us:
Saransh AI builds language and vision products for banks and insurers. Our models read
documents, route customer messages and check claim photos for millions of customers.

About the role:
We are hiring a Machine Learning Engineer to design, train and deploy models that run in
production. You will own models from the first experiment to monitoring after release.

Responsibilities:
- Train deep learning models for natural language processing and computer vision
- Turn research code into reliable services
- Deploy models with Docker and Kubernetes on AWS
- Maintain CI/CD pipelines for training and deployment
- Track experiments and monitor models in production

Requirements:
- 3+ years of experience in machine learning engineering
- Strong Python with PyTorch or TensorFlow
- scikit-learn, NumPy and Pandas
- Docker and Kubernetes
- A solid background in statistics
- Comfortable working on Linux
- Master's degree or bachelor's degree in computer science or a related field

Nice to have:
- GCP or Azure
- Spark for large datasets
- Published research or open-source work

What we offer:
- Health insurance and a yearly GPU budget for side projects
- Conference travel once a year
- Flexible working hours

How to apply:
Send your resume and a short description of a model you took to production.
