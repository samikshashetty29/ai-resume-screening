Job Title: Data Analyst
Company: Marigold Retail Pvt. Ltd.
Location: Bengaluru, India (hybrid, 3 days in office)
Employment type: Full-time

About us:
Marigold Retail runs 120 grocery and home stores across South India and a fast-growing
online shop. Our analytics team helps store, marketing and supply chain leaders make
decisions with data instead of guesswork.

About the role:
We are hiring a Data Analyst to own reporting for our sales and customer teams. You will
turn raw transaction data into clear dashboards and insights, and work closely with
managers who are not data experts.

Responsibilities:
- Build and maintain dashboards in Power BI or Tableau for sales, stock and loyalty metrics
- Write SQL queries against our PostgreSQL warehouse to answer business questions
- Automate recurring reports with Python and Pandas
- Design and analyse A/B tests for offers and promotions
- Present findings to store and marketing managers in plain language

Requirements:
- 2+ years of experience in data analysis or business intelligence
- Strong SQL and Python (Pandas, NumPy)
- Hands-on experience with Power BI or Tableau
- A good grasp of statistics
- Clear communication and problem solving skills
- Bachelor's degree in statistics, mathematics, computer science or a related field

Nice to have:
- Experience with machine learning for forecasting or segmentation
- Git and Agile ways of working
- Exposure to AWS

What we offer:
- Health insurance for you and your family
- Hybrid working and flexible hours
- A yearly learning budget of INR 40,000

How to apply:
Send your resume and a short note about a dashboard you are proud of.
