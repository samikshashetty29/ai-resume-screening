Job Title: UI/UX Designer
Company: Yatra Kit
Location: Mumbai, India (hybrid)
Employment type: Full-time

About us:
Yatra Kit is a travel planning app used by 500,000 travellers to book stays, trains and
local experiences.

About the role:
We are looking for a UI/UX Designer to shape our mobile product. You will run user
research, turn findings into wireframes and prototypes, and work closely with developers.

Responsibilities:
- Plan and run user research and usability tests
- Create wireframes, prototypes and final UI in Figma
- Grow and maintain our design system
- Work with developers until designs ship
- Share findings with product managers

Requirements:
- 2+ years of experience in product design
- Expert in Figma
- Strong UX design and UI design skills
- A portfolio showing your process
- Communication and teamwork

Nice to have:
- Adobe Photoshop or Adobe Illustrator
- HTML and CSS
- Accessibility knowledge

What we offer:
- Health insurance
- Yearly travel allowance
- Flexible hybrid working

How to apply:
Send your resume and portfolio link.
